# dm-puzzles-microsite-campaign

Nuxt 4 SPA (`ssr: false`), Vue 3 `<script setup>`, `@nuxtjs/i18n`, Firebase (Auth + Firestore, its own `dm-campaign` project, separate from Sticky Puzzles). One deployment runs many white-labelled puzzle campaigns at once, each a set number of days of puzzles with its own rules, puzzles, look and text: e.g. a charity's 30-day sponsored challenge (rolling start, a choice of tracks) or a December advent calendar (fixed dates, catch-up allowed). Started as a copy of dm-puzzles-microsite-nuxt; it keeps that repo's puzzle gameplay embedding but none of its site features. Unused microsite components, composables, stores and libs are still in `app/` and are being removed as the campaign site replaces them; don't build on them without checking they still fit.

## Working conventions

- **Comments: default to ZERO.** Do not add comments that narrate what the code does, restate identifier names, or explain why a change was made. Only a rare comment that stops a future reader silently reintroducing a bug earns its place — a single short line, never a header above each function/computed/rule. This applies to JS, Vue, CSS, and HTML. Jeremy has asked for this many times; treat justification/rationale/reason-for-change comments as banned. Explanations belong in the READMEs.
- **i18n: every user-facing string goes through `$t()`, and all 7 locale files stay in sync.** Locales: `i18n/locales/{en,fr,it,de,es,hu,pl}.json` (en is default; `strategy: 'prefix_except_default'`). Add a key to all 7. Non-English strings are machine-translated and need a native/professional review before launch — flag new ones rather than assuming they're final. The site's own strings are under `campaign.*`; a campaign's own words (title, intro…) live in its `campaign.json` `text`, read with `useCampaignText()`. Use `useLocalePath()` (bind in `<script setup>`, not template) and `useDateLocale()` for dates (NOT `useI18n().locale`).
- **CSS: one rule per selector holds everything** — base declarations, its `@media`/breakpoint/`@container` overrides, and its `:hover`/`:focus` states — nested via `&`. Never a separate `@media {…}` section or a second rule for the same selector just to hold a media query. Native nesting is supported by the Vite pipeline. Campaign colours come from `campaign.json` `theme` as CSS variables (`useCampaignTheme()`); don't hard-code a campaign's colours.
- **Tests:** `npm test` (Vitest: the day rules, the completion rule, and that every `campaigns/*/campaign.json` is valid) and `npm run test:rules` (Firestore rules in the emulator). Keep the day rules pure and tested.

## How it fits together

- **Campaigns:** `campaigns/{id}/campaign.json`, documented in `campaigns/README.md`. Only ids and hosts are bundled up front (`runtimeConfig.public.campaigns`, built in `nuxt.config.ts`); each campaign file is its own chunk loaded by `loadCampaign()` (`app/lib/campaign/registry.js`). A campaign's `hosts` send its domain's `/` to `/c/{id}`; the root lists campaigns only in dev.
- **Day rules:** `app/lib/campaign/schedule.js` (pure): `fixed` vs `rolling` schedules, `late` settings, day states `locked`/`today`/`catchup`/`missed`/`done`/`late`, `progress()`, `validateCampaign()`. Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings in the campaign's `timezone` (`app/lib/campaign/dates.js`), never the device's.
- **Progress:** `useCampaignPlayer()` in `app/composables/campaign.js` keeps `campaigns/{id}/players/{uid}` (`{track, startedAt}`) and `.../days/{day}` (`{slug, completedAt}`) live via `onSnapshot`. Both times are `serverTimestamp()` and the rules (`firestore.rules`) only allow them equal to `request.time`, and days are create-only, so a late day stays late. `account: "optional"` campaigns sign players in anonymously on Start; `"required"` ones show `CampaignSignIn.vue` (a guest who registers is upgraded with `linkWithCredential`, keeping progress). The player's profile (`{name, avatar, reason}`, `app/lib/campaign/profile.js`) is a separate `campaigns/{id}/profiles/{uid}` doc, written on registration and from `/c/{id}/profile`; avatars default to the placeholder puzzle set in `public/img/avatars` and a campaign can swap them with `profile.avatars`. The profile's `handle` is the player's public page, `/c/{id}/{handle}` (`[handle].vue`, `useCampaignPublicPage()`), backed by `campaigns/{id}/public/{handle}` whose id makes addresses unique. Players don't choose it: `saveCampaignProfile()` takes the first free one from `handleCandidates()` (name, name-2…, then random) the first time a profile is saved, batching both docs, and keeps it after that so shared links never break. Handles must avoid the reserved words in `profile.js` and `firestore.rules` (keep the two lists the same, and add any new page name under `/c/{id}/` to both). `?today=YYYY-MM-DD` previews a date with progress in sessionStorage only and no Firebase writes.
- **Landing page:** before a player starts, `/c/{id}` is the banner hero (`theme.banner`, with the tagline on it) and two columns: `CampaignExplainer.vue` ("How it works") and, on the right, the next action in a card that overlaps the banner and stays sticky through "How it works". Below that, `CampaignDetails.vue` is a tinted band with white cards for when it runs, the catch-up rule and the tracks. Both build their rows with `CampaignInfoList.vue`, from `campaign.json`. Sign-up is two steps and writes nothing to Firebase until the second is done: `CampaignSignIn.vue` keeps name, email and password in memory only (`useCampaignSignup()`), then `/c/{id}/welcome` (its own page, for tracking; reloading it loses the details and goes back to step 1) takes the avatar and reason and calls `registerPlayer()`, which creates the account and saves the profile with an automatically chosen page address. Cancel there discards everything. The same page also catches anyone signed in with no challenge in this campaign (an account from another campaign, or an abandoned old sign-up): the landing page sends them there while the campaign is open, `joinCampaign()` saves their profile and starts their challenge, and Cancel signs them out. The challenge choice is on that page too.
- **Finale:** once `progress().over`, `CampaignFinale.vue` sits above the challenge card on both the player's page and their public page: confetti (`ConfettiBurst`, once per device), the result (perfect / on time and late / or a quieter "your challenge is over" when days were missed), and for the player a share image drawn in the browser by `app/lib/campaign/certificate.js` (1080×1350 PNG in the campaign's colours, shared with the Web Share API where it takes files, otherwise downloaded). The campaign's own `text.finished` / `text.perfect` are the player's headline.
- **Challenge page:** `CampaignChallengeCard.vue` has Share (`CampaignShareMenu.vue`: link, copy, native share) and Edit pills top right, and `CampaignToday.vue` as its lead (today's puzzle, a catch-up day, or done). Players can make progress images ("15 down. 15 to go.") in `CampaignShareCard.vue`, drawn by `app/lib/campaign/shareCard.js` in three sizes: square 1080×1080 (posts), story 1080×1920 (stories, WhatsApp) and wide 1200×630 (link posts); the sentence comes from the campaign's `text.pledge` or a standard one.
- **Playing a day:** `app/pages/c/[campaign]/day/[day].vue` frames `/gameplay/{type}/index.html?json={puzzle.url}` (`Archive.getPuzzleUrl`), claims `window.channel` before the frame loads, opens a `PuzzleChannel` (`app/lib/puzzleChannel.js`) for `puzzlecomplete`, and also handles the older `{type, payload}` postMessages. `countsAsDone()` (`app/lib/campaign/completion.js`) needs 20% of words/points for word games, as they report complete when ended early. `window.puzzleApp.completion` points the completion screen back to the campaign.
- **Deploys:** push `deploy-campaigns` (see README Deployments, CLOUDFRONT.md for the rewrite function).
